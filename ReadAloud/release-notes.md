# Release Notes

## Version 1.0.0 (Initial Release)

### What's New
Initial release of Loudify - your personal text-to-speech reader.

### Features
📖 Read Anything
- Open PDF, EPUB, DOCX, TXT, HTML, and RTF files
- Paste text directly or import from URLs
- Scan printed text with camera (OCR)
- Share text from any app to Loudify

🎧 Listen Your Way
- Choose from dozens of system voices and languages
- Adjust speed and pitch to your preference
- Background playback - listen while using other apps
- Sleep timer for bedtime listening

📚 Your Reading Library
- Save documents to your personal library
- Bookmark your favorite passages
- Track your reading and listening stats
- Pick up right where you left off

🎨 Comfortable Reading
- Dark and light themes
- Clean, distraction-free interface
- Sentence highlighting as text is read

🔒 Privacy First
- All data stays on your device
- No accounts, no tracking, no ads
- Works offline with system TTS voices
