# Privacy Policy for Loudify

**Last updated: June 2026**

## Overview

Loudify is a text-to-speech reader app by Daily Apps Kit. We take your privacy seriously. This policy explains what data we collect and how we use it.

## Data Collection

Loudify collects **anonymous, aggregated usage analytics and crash reports** via Google Firebase to help us improve the app and fix bugs. **No text content, file contents, or personal information is ever sent.**

### What stays on your device (never sent anywhere):
- Text you paste or import from files
- Your reading library and bookmarks
- App preferences (voice, speed, theme)
- Reading statistics

### Anonymous Analytics & Crash Reports
- We use Firebase Analytics to count events like "playback started", "file opened", "share tapped", and "review prompted"
- We use Firebase Crashlytics to collect crash stack traces and device model when the app crashes
- **No text content, file contents, voice selections, or personal identifiers are included**
- Analytics can be disabled at any time in Settings → "Help improve Loudify"
- Firebase data is governed by [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)

### Network Access
- The app uses the device's built-in text-to-speech engine, which works offline
- Network access is used for Firebase analytics/crash/performance reporting (can be disabled in Settings)
- Google Play In-App Updates is used to check for and download new versions

## Third-Party Services

Loudify integrates the following Google LLC services:
- **Firebase Analytics** — anonymous usage events
- **Firebase Crashlytics** — anonymous crash reports and stack traces
- **Firebase Performance Monitoring** — anonymous app-startup and screen-render timing
- **Google Play In-App Updates** — checks for and downloads new versions from the Play Store

No advertising or third-party tracking SDKs are included. All collection can be disabled in Settings.

## Data Storage

All app data (library, bookmarks, settings, stats) is stored locally on your device using standard Android storage. Uninstalling the app removes all associated data.

## Children's Privacy

Loudify does not knowingly collect data from children under 13. The app contains no age-restricted content.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected in the "Last updated" date above.
