# Privacy Policy for Loudify

**Last updated: February 16, 2026**

## Overview

Loudify is a text-to-speech reader app. We take your privacy seriously. This policy explains what data we collect and how we use it.

## Data Collection

Loudify does **not** collect, store, or transmit any personal data to external servers. All your data stays on your device.

### What stays on your device:
- Text you paste or import from files
- Your reading library and bookmarks
- App preferences (voice, speed, theme)
- Reading statistics

### Camera & Photos
- Camera access is used solely for OCR text scanning
- Photos are processed on-device only
- No images are uploaded or stored beyond the scanning session

### Network Access
- The app uses the device's built-in text-to-speech engine, which works offline
- Optional Piper TTS requires a user-configured server (self-hosted)
- No analytics, tracking, or advertising SDKs are included

## Third-Party Services

Loudify does not integrate any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services.

## Data Storage

All app data is stored locally on your device using standard Android storage. Uninstalling the app removes all associated data.

## Children's Privacy

Loudify does not knowingly collect data from children under 13. The app contains no age-restricted content.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected in the "Last updated" date above.
