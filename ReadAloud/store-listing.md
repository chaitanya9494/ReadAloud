# Google Play Store Listing — Loudify

## App Name
Loudify — Read Aloud

## Short Description (80 chars max)
Turn any text, PDF, or ebook into speech. Read aloud with natural voices.

## Full Description (4000 chars max)
Loudify turns your phone into a powerful text-to-speech reader. Paste text, open files, or scan pages — Loudify reads it all aloud with natural-sounding voices.

📖 Read Anything
• Open PDF, EPUB, DOCX, TXT, HTML, and RTF files
• Paste text directly or import from URLs
• Scan printed text with your camera (OCR)
• Share text from any app to Loudify

🎧 Listen Your Way
• Choose from dozens of system voices and languages
• Adjust speed and pitch to your preference
• Background playback — listen while using other apps
• Sleep timer for bedtime listening

📚 Your Reading Library
• Save documents to your personal library
• Bookmark your favorite passages
• Track your reading and listening stats
• Pick up right where you left off

🎨 Comfortable Reading
• Dark and light themes
• Clean, distraction-free interface
• Sentence highlighting as text is read

🔒 Privacy First
• All data stays on your device
• No accounts, no tracking, no ads
• Works offline with system TTS voices

Perfect for:
• Commuters who want to "read" on the go
• Students reviewing study materials
• Anyone who prefers listening over reading
• Accessibility — making text content audible
